I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [**Department of Building Environment and Energy Engineering**](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/beee/), [**Research Institute for Smart Energy**](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/rise/), [**The Hong Kong Polytechnic University**](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/). 

I obtained my PhD degree from [**The Hong Kong Polytechnic University**](https://www.polyu.edu.hk) in 2024 supervised [Prof. Shengwei Wang](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/beee/people/academic-staff/ir-professor-wang-shengwei). Previously, I received my MEng degree from [**Tongji University**](https://www.tongji.edu.cn) in 2021 supervised by [Prof. Peng Xu](https://a434.tongji.edu.cn) and my BEng degree from [**Southwest Jiaotong University**](https://www.swjtu.edu.cn) in 2018. 

My research focuses on the intersection of **building energy efficiency and flexibility** through digitally enabled intelligent building energy systems, specifically tackling fundamental questions in control optimization and decision-making for **Grid-interactive Efficient Buildings**. I’m also broadly interested in **Built Environment**, **Energy Efficiency**, **Energy Flexibility** and **AI for Engineering**.
